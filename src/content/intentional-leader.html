Where this comes from

The place to learn what it takes to lead with greater intention.

For the better part of twenty years I've been chasing one question... what makes a leader intentional, and what happens to the people around them when they are? I've asked it in more than 250 interviews on The Intentional Leader Podcast, in 400+ live events, and in a lot of quiet one-on-ones with leaders at every level, and I've watched closely for what actually works and what doesn't. Intentional Leader is all of that, turned into 90 days you can put to work.

Here's how I define it. Intentional leadership is being deliberate, meaning what you say and do... decisive, not hesitating when it counts... and divine, leading in the way only you can. Before the 90 days are over, every leader writes their own definition, and starts living it.

Most recently we recorded our CEO season, a run of conversations with presidents and CEOs steering their organizations through big growth, and honestly it's been fascinating. From completely different directions, they kept circling back to the same truth:

The team that got you here isn't the team that gets you there.

That's the gap this program closes, with your whole leadership team in the room together. You don't need to have it all figured out to join. If you show up and do the work, you're going to make progress... we watched it happen all through our first class.

Why this exists

Over and over, the same three problems came up.

Different companies, different sizes, different titles... and the same three things keeping good leaders up at night. These are the three months of the program.

"I'm managing everything and leading nothing."

Drowning in operational noise. No clear culture, no defined language, no strategic clarity. Revenue sits untapped because nobody is being intentional about growth.

How it works

This isn't a course you log into and watch.

Most leadership training is one big day, a binder, and a lot of good intentions that are gone by Wednesday. This works the other way around... a little bit every week for 90 days, built into the work you're already doing, so it has a chance to become who you are.

Before day one, a baseline

Every leader has a short, structured coaching conversation about where they are and what they're up against. You get a snapshot of your team before we start, so we can show you what changed at the end.

Every Monday, one intention

One question: what's the one thing you'll do deliberately this week, not by drift? Answer it by text or voice note.

Every week, one episode

An episode of The Intentional Leader Podcast picked for that month's theme, short enough for the drive in. About 90% of the content is audio, so it fits a real schedule.

Every Friday, five minutes

Your win, what's stuck, how you were intentional, and your top priority for next week. Typed or spoken. It becomes the record of what's actually changing.

Once a month, live with me

A three-hour virtual session, camera on, with small breakout groups working on real problems and a workbook you'll actually use.

24/7 coaching for all 90 days

Prepping for a hard conversation at 10 pm, or trying to figure out what to hand off? Every leader has a coach on call around the clock, built on everything we teach.

Fair questions

What people ask before they sign up.

How much time does it take each week?

Most weeks, well under an hour. The episode fits on a drive, the Friday check-in takes about five minutes, and Monday's intention is one question. The only fixed block is the live session, three hours once a month.

Who should we send?

Anyone who leads people or is growing into it. The first class had owners and executives right alongside first-time supervisors, and the mix is part of what makes it work. You can send up to eight people for one price.

We're a small team. Will this still work?

Yes. Some of the best conversations in our first class came from lean teams where there was nobody to hand work off to. We built a whole segment around that, because the answer for a lean team looks different, and it's still an answer.

What is the 24/7 coaching, exactly?

It's an AI coach we built and trained on 250+ episodes of The Intentional Leader Podcast and years of our own coaching. Each leader can text it, talk to it, or chat with it any time during the 90 days to prepare for a hard conversation, think through a decision, or figure out what to delegate. It also runs the Monday and Friday check-ins. It doesn't replace the live sessions. It keeps the work going between them.

Is it all virtual?

Yes. The live sessions are on Zoom, and everything else happens on your phone. No travel, no days out of the office.

What happens after day 90?

Every company gets the Intentional Leader Report showing where your people started and where they finished. If you want to keep developing your team after that, we'll talk about what fits, and there's no pressure to continue.
